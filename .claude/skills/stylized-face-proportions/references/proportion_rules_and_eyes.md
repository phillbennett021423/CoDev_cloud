## Face proportion guide (use for QA checks)
1. Eye line = halfway between the top of the head and the chin (front AND side).
2. The middle vertical line of the head in profile = jaw/cranium split; the jaw goes forward from it; the ear sits BEHIND it.
3. Hairline to chin in thirds: brow line, nose bottom. Ear between the brow line and the nose bottom.
4. Back of the skull slants into the neck from the brow line to the nose bottom (no bulge at the back).
5. Lips: lip line at 1/3 from the nose bottom to the chin (or halfway gives the bottom lip).
6. Profile: eye corner to front of the eye = front of the eye to front of the face = front of the face to the nose tip. A vertical from the face front meets the chin (varies by style).
7. Slant from the nose tip to the jaw (mouth sits back).
8. Eye spacing = 1 eye width. The nose wing aligns with the inner eye corner.
9. Mouth corners never wider than the iris centre (midpoint of the eye).

## Eyes (JMStsdAis3g)
- Wrong way: digging sockets with the Draw brush with no plan. Right way: annotation from the top to the bottom of the head, mid = eye line, Crease Polish it, grab the line back (eyes in sockets), smooth to get a brow line, Scrape the temples.
- UV Sphere rotated X 90, shade smooth, Mirror (mirror object = head). Eyelids form where the eye intersects the head; remesh 0.02; Draw brush for the upper/lower lids, then grab. The eye slopes back from the side and the inner corner goes in. Scrape a flat plane under the eye. Crease Polish with Ctrl for the lid edges. Remesh 0.01, then re-crease and grab so the lids wrap the eyeball.
- Iris preview: edit-mode select the front vertex, Ctrl + grow; vertex paint with the paint mask, black iris; shading colour = Attribute. Rotate the eyes slightly for a natural look. Add box-modelled brows/lashes early.
