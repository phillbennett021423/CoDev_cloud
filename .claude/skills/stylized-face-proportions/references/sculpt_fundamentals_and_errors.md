# Sculpt fundamentals + coaching videos
(BCcflb85NB4 real-time bust, BRT88HXmZkA fixing student sculpts, KP_DqpOIe5U beginner bust, QvrMFSPGVLc fixing ugly model, U_MIhJM--Fk animation character breakdown, cq-oe1n5AdM head planes, 1SPWv8PzYGg practice, ZdybynVI_t0 live coaching)

Setup: viewport focal length 150 mm for heads (115 mm for full-body blockouts). Second viewport/image editor with the reference always open. Round Cube with 14 divisions as the head start (Q favourites). X symmetry on for the head, OFF for hair detail/cloth folds/feet.
Head order: cranium, then pull the jaw down (grab), Line Project to flatten/slice, Crease Polish to mark the jaw/cranium split, neck and ear positions. Neck is NOT vertical (leans forward). The back of the head flows into the neck. Slice off the unused half of the bust with Line Project.
Planes first: block the head planes with Scrape (eye plane, forehead, temporal/side planes, cheek, jaw) before any feature. Temple comes slightly forward. Eyes sit INSIDE sockets. Three key eyelid edges; lids wrap the eyeball. The mouth works like eyelids and wraps around the teeth; lower lip sits behind the upper lip. In profile the nose projects the most.
Remesh ladder: 0.05 -> 0.04 -> 0.03 -> 0.02 -> 0.01 (R shows the voxel grid, Ctrl R applies). Too-high resolution = noisy surface; drop back to 0.01 and smooth with Clay Strips + Shift smooth.
Features: nose = separate Round Cube kept triangular, merged and blended with Clay Strips; nostrils with the Draw brush; Crease Polish on the nose sides. Eyelids = Draw brush over the eye, then Grab to shape, then Crease Polish with Ctrl for the lid edges. Lips: Crease Polish the mouth line (small radius), Clay Strips for lip volumes, Inflate to round. Ears: grab into shape before rotating; Symmetrize direction +X to -X.
Brows on sculpts: Plane rotated X 90, Mirror + Solidify, dark viewport colour, shaped per-vertex. Eyelash strip the same way.
Paint: face-select masking to paint regions; colour copy with Ctrl C / Ctrl V.
Hair blockout: mask, Extract, sculpt; Draw-brush strand groups (symmetry off).
Common student errors: no reference; mouth-to-chin distance too long; no temple region; jaw too close to the ear; weak cheekbones and jaw definition; surface noise. Fix order: proportions, then planes, then features.
Animation breakdown (37 h total): concept first, then sculpt, hands (cube palm + skin-mod fingers), feet (one toe Alt D x4), retopo (Slide Relax brush, problem-solve hand loops), clothes (Multires folds), hair GN (Set Hair Curves Profile, Duplicate Hair Curves, Clump), Rigify + pose test, UV seams in hidden areas, texture with a highlight colour pass, a mouth-open shape key while texturing so no spots are missed, procedural roughness, check in Material Preview AND Cycles, eye-close shape key (edit + sculpt mode) for animation.
Practice: timed speed sculpts of 1–2 h; volume over perfection.
Game/bpy relevance: these are QA criteria for image-to-3D cleanup. After generating a head, check the profile (nose most forward), the temple, the eye-socket depth, lid wrap, lip overlap, the neck angle and the mouth-chin distance on the 4-view renders.
