# Free 4 h sculpting course (7-D0kPTkmpg), faces from references (M8wzKrQG5gM), how I sculpt faces (8za06nMEjBE), sculpting explained (w4wsV8mlF3o)
These confirm the masterclass/head notes; new or reinforced points only:
- Course order: ch1 tools + add-ons (Extra Mesh Objects, Round Cube + Mirror + Solidify in Quick Favourites), ch2 follow-along bust, ch3 fundamentals, ch4 full body to final render. Lens 150 (80–100 recommended for beginners; 50 is too wide). Split view: clean progress view + working view + reference image editor.
- Round Cube divisions **14** for the head, fewer (about 8) for ears/neck/torso blocks. Neck = round cube scaled in Z and **rotated back**; torso round cube; ears symmetrized +X to -X.
- **Backup**: duplicate the blockout into a hidden "blockout backup" collection before detailing (same in M8wz).
- Remesh ladder in practice: 0.05/0.04 blockout -> 0.03 -> 0.02 lids/nose -> 0.01 final face; drop BACK to a lower value whenever big shapes must change (low poly = easier big edits). Join parts (Ctrl J) then remesh together at the current level.
- Faces from references (M8wz): think of the **Loomis method**: egg from front, side and top, slice the sides flat (Line Project), push the subject's distinctive feature early (e.g. a massive jaw), flat chin planes; rotate the head to match the reference angle, use the **Copy Attributes** add-on (Ctrl C) to copy rotation/location from the head to new parts (nose). Nose = triangle from the front.
- Hair placeholder (course ch4): Mask Extract with offset -0.5 and thickness up, edge crease 0.5 (Shift E) + Subsurf for clean locks, Solidify 0.05 (scene scale) on shell pieces, remesh hair at 0.02–0.05 then Scrape.
- Sculpting explained (w4ws): blockout first, never a sphere, until anatomy is second nature. Retopo only if animated or for games (static renders can stay high poly). Practice order: head planes -> face -> blockouts of many body types -> organic shapes (hair, cloth folds) -> anatomy studies. Learn by: follow a tutorial, redo it WITHOUT the tutorial, then a similar own project.
