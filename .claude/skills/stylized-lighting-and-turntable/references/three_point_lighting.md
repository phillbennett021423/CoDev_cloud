## 3-point lighting (presentation)
- Backdrop: plane, extrude the back edge up, Bevel modifier, segments 10, shade smooth.
- Cycles, overlays off, World strength 0 while lighting.
- Key Area light to the side: shadows convey the forms; big = soft, small = hard. Fill: duplicate, Shift T aims at the cursor, lower power, bigger. Rim: new Area light behind, power ~100 (scene-scale), warm colour, S Z to stretch over the whole body. **Light linking**: Object > Shading > Light Linking > new collection, drop the backdrop in, uncheck to EXCLUDE it from the rim.
- Extra backdrop light behind the character towards the backdrop (separation); a coloured backdrop (dark blue) looks good.
- Camera: Align Active Camera to View, 1200 x 1500 (Instagram), lock camera to view, focal 150, Max Samples 150.
